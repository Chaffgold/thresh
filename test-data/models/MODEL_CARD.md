# Test-data models

These ONNX/SafeTensors artifacts are **random-weight stubs** used only to verify
shape contracts in CI — they are not trained models and produce meaningless
outputs. Real trained checkpoints replace them once their track's exit criterion
is met (see `openspec/changes/flight-data-training-pipeline/`); the training and
export recipes are in [`TRAINING.md`](../../TRAINING.md).

> **Data lineage / ADS-B posture.** When trained, both checkpoints learn from
> *synthetic perception driven by real ADS-B truth*: a real OpenSky trajectory
> is the truth target and `thresh-synth` synthesises the sensor returns. ADS-B
> is consumed as **system-level truth, never as a measurement** (design
> Decision 4). The Track B classifier trains on classical-tracker filter
> outputs, not on raw ADS-B state. OpenSky attribution and the ADSBx
> non-redistribution posture are in [`LICENSING.md`](../../LICENSING.md).
> **Checkpoint release requires documented rights** ("Trained models" in
> `LICENSING.md`). Before replacing a stub with a checkpoint trained with
> OpenSky-derived truth, record the dataset/version, applicable terms or written
> authorization, and the basis for the intended training and distribution rights.
> A research/evaluation label alone does not establish distribution permission.

## `imm_mode_classifier.onnx` (Track B IMM mode classifier)

- **Input** `features`: `(1, 10, 13)` float32 — 10 consecutive 13-dim
  filter-state projections `[x, vx, y, vy, z, vz, Pxx, Pvxvx, Pyy, Pvyvy, Pzz,
  Pvzvz, elapsed_seconds]` from `thresh_filter::imm::project_filter_state`
  (`CLASSIFIER_FEATURE_DIM = 13`). Features MUST come from a tracker filter
  state, **never** from a raw ADS-B / measurement vector (design Decision 4).
- **Output** `mode_probs`: `(1, 4)` float32 — softmaxed probabilities over
  `[CV, CA, CTRV, coord_turn]` (index-aligned to `ImmConfig::cv_ca_ctrv_ct`).
- **Provenance:** random weights (`np.random.default_rng(42)`), structurally a
  time-mean + linear + softmax. Replaced by the `python/training` + `python/export`
  pipeline output once the Track B exit criterion (task 6.8: held-out accuracy
  ≥ 0.70, MOTA no worse than analytic) is met; until then the `learned-imm`
  feature is **off by default**.
- **Timing contract (Decision 30, 2026-09-22):** record post-birth measurement
  updates, including tentative tracks, but not births or coasting. First interval
  is zero; later intervals accumulate all prediction time since the preceding
  update, including missed measurements. Windows do not reset that value.
  The regenerated stub still uses only random weights, not training data.
  Legacy 12-wide models/datasets are incompatible and explicitly rejected.
  The training exporter supports dynamic batch; this tiny test stub fixes batch=1.

## `test_detector.onnx` (Track A detector)

- **Input** `point_cloud`: `(1, 1000, 4)` float32 `[x, y, z, intensity]` in the
  sensor-centred ENU frame (design Decision 10).
- **Outputs:** `boxes (1, 100, 7)` float32 `[x, y, z, L, W, H, yaw]`,
  `scores (1, 100, 1)` float32, `classes (1, 100, 1)` int64 — class index in
  `[0, 5)` per the taxonomy in `python/training/classes.py` (design Decision 8).
- **Provenance:** random weights (`np.random.default_rng(42)`), a small
  MLP + per-output heads (`scripts/generate_test_model.py`). The trained
  3DETR-style model (`python/training/detector_model.py`) replaces it once the
  Track A exit criterion (task 7.9 / Decision 26: distance-AP above the classical
  baseline, matched class accuracy ≥ 0.50, and downstream MOTA above the stub) is
  met. `test_weights.safetensors` is the analogous stub for the pure-Rust
  SafeTensors decoder path.
