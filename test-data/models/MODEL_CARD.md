# Test-data models

These ONNX/SafeTensors artifacts are **random-weight stubs** used only to verify
shape contracts in CI — they are not trained models and produce meaningless
outputs. Real trained checkpoints replace them once their track's exit criterion
is met (see `openspec/changes/flight-data-training-pipeline/`). A full model card
(provenance, training data, metrics) lands in Phase 10.

| File | Contract | Generated by | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `test_detector.onnx` | `(1,1000,4)` → boxes `(1,100,7)` + scores `(1,100,1)` | `scripts/generate_test_model.py` | Random-weight stub (Track A) |
| `test_weights.safetensors` | native DETR decoder weights | `scripts/generate_test_weights.py` | Random-weight stub (Track A) |
| `imm_mode_classifier.onnx` | `(batch,10,12)` → softmax `mode_probs (batch,4)` | `scripts/generate_imm_classifier_stub.py` | Random-weight stub (Track B) |

## `imm_mode_classifier.onnx` (Track B IMM mode classifier)

- **Input** `features`: `(batch, 10, 12)` float32 — 10 consecutive 12-dim
  filter-state projections `[x, vx, y, vy, z, vz, Pxx, Pvxvx, Pyy, Pvyvy, Pzz,
  Pvzvz]` from `thresh_filter::imm::project_filter_state`
  (`CLASSIFIER_FEATURE_DIM = 12`). Features MUST come from a tracker filter
  state, **never** from a raw ADS-B / measurement vector (design Decision 4).
- **Output** `mode_probs`: `(batch, 4)` float32 — softmaxed probabilities over
  `[CV, CA, CTRV, coord_turn]` (index-aligned to `ImmConfig::cv_ca_ctrv_ct`).
- **Provenance:** random weights (`np.random.default_rng(42)`), structurally a
  time-mean + linear + softmax. Replaced by the `python/training` + `python/export`
  pipeline output once the Track B exit criterion (task 6.8) is met; until then
  the `learned-imm` feature is **off by default**.
